About us

Kazan Neft is one of the leading oil companies in the Republic of Tatarstan, Russia. Founded with the goal of efficiently and sustainably developing energy resources, the company has grown rapidly and now operates strategically across the Russian territory.

Over the years, Kazan Neft has established itself as a reference in the oil and gas sector by continuously investing in technology, automation, and innovation. With a strong commitment to operational excellence and sustainable development, the company not only meets today’s energy demands but is also preparing for the challenges of the future.
